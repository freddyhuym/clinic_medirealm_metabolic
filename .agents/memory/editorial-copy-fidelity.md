---
name: Editorial copy fidelity
description: How to handle user-supplied knowledge-base article copy.
---

Publish user-supplied article copy verbatim. Preserve wording, ordering, labels, hashtags, and paragraph boundaries; do not summarize, rewrite, “clean up,” or omit apparent CMS labels unless explicitly instructed.

**Why:** The supplied wording and structure are approved editorial content, including text that may look accidental or unusual.

**How to apply:** When adding or updating knowledge-base articles, convert formatting into the site’s structured blocks without changing the visible text or its sequence.
